Color & Material Consultation

The right combination of colours and materials can completely transform the personality of an interior. At Nestora, we help create thoughtful colour palettes and material combinations that bring harmony, warmth, and character to every space. We carefully consider the size of the room, natural lighting, furniture, existing elements, textures, and the overall mood that the space should create before selecting the final palette.

From soft neutral tones and natural textures to deeper shades and refined finishes, every choice is made with the complete interior in mind. We guide you through materials such as wood, stone, fabrics, tiles, wall finishes, and decorative surfaces, ensuring that each element works together beautifully. Our approach creates interiors that feel cohesive, timeless, and personal rather than simply following temporary trends.

  • Personalised colour palette selection
  • Material and texture coordination
  • Wall, flooring and surface finish guidance
  • Balanced and timeless interior combinations
