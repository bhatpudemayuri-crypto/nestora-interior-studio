Lighting Design

Lighting plays an important role in defining the atmosphere, comfort, and character of an interior. At Nestora, we carefully plan lighting solutions that work beautifully with the overall design of a space. We consider natural light, room proportions, furniture placement, colours, materials, and the everyday activities of the people using the space. Our goal is to create lighting that feels warm, balanced, practical, and visually comfortable throughout the day.

From soft ambient lighting that creates a calm and welcoming mood to focused lighting that highlights artwork, textures, furniture, and architectural details, every element is planned with purpose. We create lighting schemes that not only make a room functional but also enhance its beauty and personality. Whether it is a peaceful bedroom, an elegant living room, a productive workspace, or a sophisticated commercial interior, the right lighting helps bring the entire design together.

  • Warm and balanced ambient lighting
  • Focused accent lighting for interiors
  • Functional lighting for everyday activities
  • Lighting plans based on space and natural light
